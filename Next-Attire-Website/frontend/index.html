Our Journey

Experience built our foundation.

Purpose drives our future.

Drawing on over two decades of industry experience, we launched NEXT ATTIRE in 2020 with a vision to create contemporary menswear that blends quality, comfort, and timeless style.

The brand reflects our commitment to innovation, craftsmanship, and delivering exceptional value to modern consumers.

In May 2024, with the formation of Nathji Agencies Pvt. Ltd., we made a strategic decision to exit the distribution business and focus exclusively on building and growing Next Attire.

Today, Next Attire represents the culmination of years of industry knowledge, customer trust, and a passion for creating modern menswear designed for the aspirations of today's generation.
